          How to review a deck

          1. Add the deck and its current Excel files, then select Start review.
          2. Open a finding to read why it was flagged. Select Inspect to compare the slide and the workbook cell. A possible source still needs your review.
          3. Record your decision and notes on the finding. Open Download reports and review notes to export the results.
